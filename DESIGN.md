# DOVET design direction

DOVET is an evidence post: a carrier pigeon brings a small, legible receipt from a public market source. The product is for people who want to inspect and retain an observation of a Solana token without connecting a wallet. Postal sorting, delivery and filing connect the identity directly to the working flow.

## References inspected

The supplied reference archive was inspected at `work/imported-crypto-references/WORKSPACE-CRIPTO-PARA-AMIGOS/referencias`. The review included SIFT, BEACON and CLASP `DESIGN.md` files, their UI/motion source, and their first marketing artworks. This is a source-and-asset study, not a claim that those reference applications were browser-tested during documentation.

| Reference | Specific aspect informing DOVET |
| --- | --- |
| SIFT | Its open workroom, small stage labels and tangible report output keep rule-based work understandable. DOVET uses a compact delivery route and an inspectable receipt, with one original bird and postal imagery. |
| BEACON | Its clear distinction between provider status, failed fetches and recorded execution informs honest unavailable/partial states and the separation between scheduled configuration and observed runs. The identity here uses a light postal office rather than a dark signal landscape. |
| CLASP | Its deliberate pixel formation, reduced-motion bypass, focus-aware interactions and consistent identity across site and films establish the motion quality bar. DOVET's native shutter, bird delivery and paper arrival form a different short sequence. |

No reference characters, logos, source footage or financial mechanisms are presented as DOVET's own. The current product does not inherit the references' trading, lending or wallet capabilities.

## Identity and typography

- Cream `#f6f1e5` is paper and the main ground.
- Plum `#24152d` is ink, structural rules and the bird outline.
- Vermilion `#cf493a` identifies the messenger bag and marketing accents. UI commands use the deeper `#bd3d30` token.
- Lilac `#dad1e7` provides restrained sorting and window accents.
- Silkscreen supplies the wordmark and compact stamps; IBM Plex Mono supplies working labels, timestamps and evidence; Space Grotesk supplies the website's prose and editorial headings.

The original bird has ivory feathers, an alert eye, a stepped plum outline, a short angular tail and a red satchel carrying a receipt. It remains recognizable in transparent, avatar, banner, site and film formats. Generated PNG source files are preserved in `marketing/raw/`; prompts and export notes are documented alongside them.

## Composition and journeys

The observation desk is visible immediately below the masthead. Its mint entry and small postal-window scene lead into the route, actual receipt and three-asset public table. The main action is not hidden behind a long marketing page.

The receipt is the central document: reading, source receipts and recorded task events occupy separate tabs. Source details expose endpoint, retrieval time, HTTP result, byte count and SHA-256. JSON export lets the user keep the same report outside the browser.

The archive distinguishes this device from the public route and supports filtered review. Field notes explain the route, source limitations, daily schedule, local retention and unimplemented token direction. Marketing downloads are kept outside the product navigation.

Mobile stacks the working form and scene, simplifies the public rows and converts receipt metrics to readable lines. Long addresses and hashes wrap. There is no 3D scene, nested dashboard-card system or fake balance display.

## Motion and state

`mountIntro` in `src/intro.js` opens a postal shutter, carries an envelope across READ / COMPARE / KEEP, and reveals a receipt over approximately 3.28 seconds. It runs once per tab session, can be replayed, includes Skip and Escape, restores focus, and bypasses animation when reduced motion is requested. This native DOM/CSS sequence is explicitly introductory, not a video or server progress meter.

During a lookup the website shows a general request-in-flight state. Detailed task events become available when the server report returns. Decorative character motion must not imply individual task completion before evidence exists. The three eight-second marketing films are separate illustrative motion graphics and are labeled as such.

## Truthful product states

- A single reading is a baseline; a percentage change needs a compatible earlier observation.
- Missing pools and source failures remain visible; zero is distinct from unavailable.
- A matched second source must identify the same pool and token orientation.
- Source-response hashes fingerprint content; they do not establish authenticity or token safety.
- The public schedule is daily at 03:17 UTC. A configured schedule and an observed automatic execution are different states.
- Custom receipts are on demand and retained locally up to 30. They are not added to the public route.
- The public feed exposes up to 14 runs; development storage is capped at 180 runs. Hosted Blob object deletion is not currently scheduled.
- Official X: https://x.com/DovetSol. No project wallet or confirmed CA has been supplied. The public address state is CA: soon.
- Future token-sponsored observation capacity is a direction only. No token purchase is needed to use the tool.

## Verification criteria

Check legible hierarchy at 390px and desktop widths; intact bird alpha; focus visibility; functioning receipt tabs, source links, exports and archive filters; unavailable and partial data; repeated lookups; Skip/Escape and reduced motion; and public schedule labels based on real status fields. Screen captures and actual browser interactions are required before claiming visual QA passed.
